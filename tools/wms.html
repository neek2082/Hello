Warehouse Practice (WMS)

A WMS (warehouse management system) is the computer system a warehouse uses to track every carton that comes in and goes out. Here, six students share one practice warehouse. Each of you receives 3 deliveries and ships 3 store orders, and everyone sees the stock change live.

Students: join your class

Enter the code from your lecturer. You'll be given a warehouse and your orders.

Use the same name each day so your work stays together.

Lecturers: start a class

Students can only join and work between the first and last lesson day.
