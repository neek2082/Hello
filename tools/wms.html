Warehouse Practice (WMS)

A WMS (warehouse management system) is the computer system a warehouse uses to track every carton that comes in and goes out. Here, six students share one practice warehouse. Each of you receives and puts away 3 purchase orders, then picks, packs and ships 3 sales orders, and everyone sees the stock in every bin change live.

Sign in

Students and lecturers: use the username and password on the slip from your lecturer. Students go straight to their own warehouse.

On a shared computer, sign out when you finish. Your work is saved either way.

    WMS: all warehouses

    Each student account has a fixed place: Students 001–006 are Warehouse 1 (places 1–6), 007–012 Warehouse 2, and so on. Each place has its own 3 purchase orders and 3 sales orders, the same in every warehouse: 21 steps in all (receive and put away 6 lines, pick 6 lines, pack and ship 3 orders). Students see system messages, not scores; refused entries are counted here and in the CSV.

      The downloads open in Google Sheets or Excel, for grading. Reset clears every warehouse, ready for the next class: download the results first if you need them.
