How is this worked out?
  • Cost points = lowest site cost ÷ this site's cost × 5. The cheapest site gets 5; dearer sites get less.
  • Importance % = a factor's importance level ÷ all importance levels added together.
  • Other-factor points = (rating × importance %) for each factor, added together.
  • Total = cost points × cost % + other-factor points × other-factors %. The highest total wins.
