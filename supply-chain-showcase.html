How it works

How each product travels

Good to know

  • Batches: factories make items in batches. If you order 1 juice, the factory still makes 24 — the extras wait at the Distribution Centre, so the next juice orders are collected much faster.
  • Trucks wait until they are full, or until they have waited a few hours, before leaving.
  • Ships leave the Export Port once a day and carry every product together. The sea trip takes about 2½ days.
  • Speed: use the speed slider (1 = slow, 10 = fast). With no orders waiting, everything stops.
  • Blue number on each place: how many items are there right now — waiting, or being made or handled.
  • Phone ordering: scan the QR code on the ocean with a phone camera. Each tap on the phone is one order, and it shows up here within a second.
  • Only the factory reacts to orders. Everything after it simply passes items along as soon as it can.
